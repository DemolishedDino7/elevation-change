How it works

Every game gets a score. Wins earn points for opponent quality, a win bonus that scales with the opponent, and a margin bonus with diminishing returns. Losses to elite teams earn real credit if they're close — a near-upset of a ranked team is worth more than most wins over bad ones. Losses to bad teams cost more the wider they get, and a 28-point-plus loss to a non-elite team takes an extra hit. FCS wins are capped low. FCS losses start at −35 before margin. Recent games count noticeably more — last week is worth 100%, the week before 82%, and it keeps sliding — but nothing ages out. The season score is the sum, and ties break on quality wins, strength of wins, quality losses, fewer bad losses, point differential and the most recent result — never on record. Byes never cost a team: each bye week counts as that team's average game, and a team on a bye moves at most 5 spots, unless teams it beat earlier are re-rated weaker. Every week is judged with opponent strength as it stood that week.

Judged against expectations. Before every game, The Bot sets an expected margin using only what was known at kickoff, and each result is graded against it. An upset win as the underdog earns a bonus that grows with the size of the upset. Winning by far less than expected (an unconvincing win) counts for less, though a win never costs points. Losing as the favorite is an upset loss and a real penalty. A quality loss only goes to the underdog that played the game at least 3 points closer than expected. An underdog that loses by about as much as expected gets an expected loss, and one that loses by 14 or more beyond expectations gets a blowout loss. A loss never adds points on its own.

This is the same formula as the Western 20, run across all of FBS. It's a résumé ranking: it rewards what a team has done, and it will disagree with The Bot's power ratings, which measure how strong a team is going forward. Everything here is computed by Elevation Change. Results and box-score stats (yards per play, third downs, explosive plays, turnovers, sacks) are calculated from play-by-play, and how good each opponent is comes from The Bot's current national ratings, not the AP poll or ESPN. FCS opponents are placed where their rating would rank among FBS teams. Rankings update automatically after every weekend.
