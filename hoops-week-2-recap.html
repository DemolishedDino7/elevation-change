The Western 23: Week 2 Basketball Recap

Basketball weekly recaps are here. Gonzaga takes down No. 8 Purdue, Nevada edges Saint Mary’s in overtime, Northern Illinois steals one at Dayton, and Boise State hits triple digits twice.

By Tyler Angerbauer | Nov 9, 2026 | 23 teams, six conferences

The first full week is in the books, and basketball is back.

The Western 23 provides a look at what the next level of college basketball programs have to face: significant turnover, restructuring, and conference realignment is found in nearly every team we cover.

This recap is the first of a weekly series Elevation Change will publish that will highlight what each team has brought to the court in the past week rather than the similar Western 20 recaps we do for football that goes through each team’s previous games in detail.

With that being said, let’s get started and see how the Western 23 showcased last week.

Pac-12

Gonzaga

Season 2–0 | This week 2–0

Best game
78–72 win vs. No. 8 Purdue
Games and results
  • Nov. 2: W 78–72 vs. No. 8 Purdue (Las Vegas)
  • Nov. 7: W 109–68 vs. New Mexico State

Gonzaga could hardly have asked for a better start to its inaugural Pac-12 campaign. The No. 15 Bulldogs opened the season with a statement victory over No. 8 Purdue in Las Vegas, defeating the Boilermakers 78–72 in one of the opening week’s premier matchups. Mark Few’s squad followed the victory with a dominant 109–68 performance against New Mexico State, surpassing the century mark in its home opener. The Zags have quickly established themselves as one of the conference’s strongest teams, pairing an impressive ranked victory with an explosive offensive showing. With an experienced coaching staff and plenty of talent, Gonzaga’s early results suggest another season of national relevance could be on the horizon.

Utah State

Season 2–0 | This week 2–0

Best game
94–58 win vs. Idaho State
Games and results
  • Nov. 2: W 94–58 vs. Idaho State
  • Nov. 7: W 90–56 vs. Omaha

Utah State began the Ben Jacobson era with a pair of comfortable victories, taking down Idaho State and Omaha by a combined 70 points. The Aggies opened the season with a 94–58 win over the Bengals before defeating the Mavericks 90–56 five days later. While neither matchup presented a major test, Utah State’s ability to control both games from start to finish provides an encouraging sign for a program navigating its transition into the Pac-12. The Aggies have displayed offensive consistency while limiting opponents to fewer than 60 points in both contests. More challenging opponents await, but Utah State has given fans in Logan plenty to be optimistic about after the first week.

Oregon State

Season 2–0 | This week 2–0

Best game
96–68 win vs. California Baptist
Games and results
  • Nov. 2: W 102–61 vs. Weber State
  • Nov. 6: W 96–68 vs. California Baptist

The Justin Joyner era at Oregon State is off to an impressive start, with the Beavers picking up two dominant victories at Gill Coliseum. Oregon State opened the season by dismantling Weber State 102–61, demonstrating an explosive offense capable of overwhelming its opponents. The Beavers followed that performance with a 96–68 victory over California Baptist, improving to 2–0 while averaging 99 points per game. Oregon State has shown early promise on the offensive end, a welcome development for a program looking to establish itself in the restructured Pac-12. Although the level of competition will increase significantly, the Beavers’ encouraging start gives Joyner a strong foundation to build upon.

Boise State

Season 2–0 | This week 2–0

Best game
100–81 win vs. Butler
Games and results
  • Nov. 4: W 132–53 vs. West Coast Baptist
  • Nov. 8: W 100–81 vs. Butler

Boise State wasted little time showing off its offensive potential in the new season. The Broncos opened their campaign with an overwhelming 132–53 victory against West Coast Baptist, scoring more points than any other team in the Pac-12 during its opening game. Leon Rice’s team then faced a considerably stronger opponent in Butler, but continued its impressive offensive start with a 100–81 victory. The Broncos have now reached triple digits in both games, providing an encouraging sign for a team hoping to challenge the conference’s established powers. Although the opening competition has varied considerably in difficulty, Boise State’s ability to generate offense has been one of the more noteworthy developments of the first week.

Colorado State

84–74 win vs. Montana State
Games and results
  • Nov. 2: W 84–74 vs. Montana State
  • Nov. 6: W 78–67 vs. Idaho

Colorado State opened its first Pac-12 season with a pair of double-digit victories over regional opponents. The Rams began with an 84–74 win against Montana State before defeating Idaho 78–67 later in the week. Colorado State’s balanced offensive performances provide an encouraging foundation as the Rams prepare for a more demanding conference schedule. With Gonzaga, Utah State and San Diego State among the teams expected to compete near the top of the Pac-12, the Rams will need to continue developing consistency if they hope to challenge for a conference championship.

Texas State

Season 2–0 | This week 2–0

Best game
67–55 win vs. Texas A&M–Corpus Christi
Games and results
  • Nov. 2: W 67–55 vs. Texas A&M–Corpus Christi
  • Nov. 5: W 135–66 vs. Howard Payne

Texas State began its first season as a Pac-12 member with two victories, although the Bobcats’ offensive performances could hardly have been more different. The Bobcats opened with a defensive-minded 67–55 win against Texas A&M–Corpus Christi before exploding for 135 points in a 69-point victory over Division III Howard Payne. The enormous scoring outburst provides an early highlight for a program facing a significant increase in competition following its move from the Sun Belt. Texas State has shown the ability to win through different styles, but whether that versatility translates against stronger Division I opponents remains to be seen. For now, the Bobcats have started their historic campaign on a positive note.

102–78 win vs. Cal State Fullerton
Games and results

San Diego State opened its 2026–27 campaign with a convincing 102–78 victory over Cal State Fullerton, beginning its first Pac-12 season on a positive note. The Aztecs surpassed the century mark in their season opener, providing an encouraging offensive performance for a program traditionally recognized for its defensive intensity. Brian Dutcher’s team enters the season with aspirations of challenging Gonzaga and Utah State near the top of the conference standings, and its opening victory offers an early glimpse of that potential. While one game provides only a limited picture of the Aztecs’ capabilities, reaching 102 points suggests San Diego State could have the offensive firepower necessary to complement its established defensive identity.

Games and results

Washington State’s return to Pac-12 basketball began on a disappointing note, as the Cougars suffered a 76–61 home defeat to Davidson in their season opener. Mountain West

New Mexico

Season 2–0 | This week 2–0

Best game
100–60 win vs. Utah Tech
Games and results
  • Nov. 2: W 100–60 vs. Utah Tech
  • Nov. 7: W 94–65 vs. Northern Arizona

New Mexico entered the season as the Mountain West preseason favorite and wasted little time demonstrating why. The Lobos opened their campaign with a dominant 100–60 victory over Utah Tech before handling Northern Arizona 94–65 to improve to 2–0. Eric Olen’s squad averaged 97 points across its first two games, establishing an impressive offensive rhythm while comfortably controlling both matchups. The Lobos also demonstrated defensive effectiveness, limiting opponents to an average of 62.5 points. Although New Mexico has yet to face another conference contender, its opening performances provide plenty of reasons for optimism in Albuquerque. With several traditional Mountain West powers now competing in the Pac-12, the Lobos appear determined to take advantage of their opportunity to lead the new conference.

Nevada

Season 2–0 | This week 2–0

Best game
96–95 overtime win vs. Saint Mary’s
Games and results
  • Nov. 2: W 86–49 vs. Robert Morris
  • Nov. 8: W 96–95 vs. Saint Mary’s (OT, Lake Tahoe Classic)

Nevada delivered one of the most impressive victories of the opening week, taking down Saint Mary’s 96–95 in overtime at the Lake Tahoe Classic. The thrilling victory came after the Wolf Pack opened their season with a dominant 86–49 win over Robert Morris, giving Steve Alford’s squad a perfect start to his final campaign in Reno. The win against Saint Mary’s is particularly significant given the Gaels’ national reputation and consistent postseason success. After cruising through their opener, the Wolf Pack proved capable of winning a close contest against a quality opponent. Nevada entered the season hoping to challenge New Mexico and Grand Canyon for the Mountain West championship, and its opening-week performances have only strengthened that possibility.

Games and results

UTEP’s return to the Mountain West began with a disappointing 84–76 loss to Northern Kentucky in its season opener. The Miners struggled to contain the Norse offensively, allowing 84 points in a game that highlighted some of the challenges facing Joe Golding’s rebuilt roster. With numerous new contributors adjusting to their roles, UTEP will need time to develop the consistency necessary to compete in its new conference. Cam McDaniels helped the Miners secure their first win of the season with 27 points and 40% from three. If McDaniels can continue to showcase that level of skill, the Miners could potentially play spoiler in a fairly competitive Mountain West conference.

95–74 win vs. UTSA
Games and results
  • Nov. 2: W 95–74 vs. UTSA

San Jose State opened its season with a convincing 95–74 victory over UTSA, offering an encouraging start for a program looking to improve on last year’s nine-win campaign. Games and results
  • Nov. 2: L 87–89 vs. Grambling State (neutral site)
  • Nov. 5: L 75–94 at Tulsa

Northern Illinois experienced one of the more surprising opening weeks among Elevation Change’s covered programs, overcoming two difficult defeats to earn an impressive road victory against Dayton. The Huskies began their inaugural Horizon League season with a heartbreaking 89–87 loss to Grambling State before falling 94–75 at Tulsa. However, Matt Majkrzak’s team responded in impressive fashion, defeating Dayton 81–76 in overtime on the Flyers’ home court. The victory provides an encouraging sign for a program that won just nine games last season and was picked near the bottom of its new conference. Although defensive consistency remains a concern after allowing an average of 86.3 points through three contests, Northern Illinois has already demonstrated an ability to compete against quality competition.

Big West

Sacramento State

Season 1–1 | This week 1–1

Best game
77–83 loss at Santa Clara
Games and results
  • Nov. 2: W 90–74 vs. Jessup
  • Nov. 7: L 77–83 at Santa Clara

Sacramento State began its first season in the Big West with a competitive opening week under head coach Mike Bibby. The Hornets opened their campaign with a 90–74 victory over Jessup before traveling to Santa Clara for their first significant test. Sacramento State pushed the Broncos throughout the contest and held a 67–60 second-half advantage, but a decisive 15–0 Santa Clara run turned the game around and handed the Hornets an 83–77 defeat. Mikey Williams led Sacramento State with 21 points in the loss, providing an encouraging sign for a team hoping to develop one of the conference’s more explosive offenses. Although the Hornets finished the week with a split record, their competitive performance against Santa Clara suggests they could be more dangerous than their preseason projections indicate.
