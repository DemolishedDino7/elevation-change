How it works

The bracket uses the playoff rules The Bot simulates: the SEC, Big Ten, ACC and Big 12 champions get automatic bids, along with the highest-ranked champion from the Group of Six. Notre Dame gets in if it's in the top 12. Every other spot goes to the next-highest teams in the Index. Each conference's champion is its highest-ranked team in the Index right now. The four highest-ranked teams in the field get the byes, and the rest are seeded straight down the Index. It's a résumé bracket, so it shows who has earned a spot, not who would win.

The conference odds come from The Bot's power ratings, which measure how strong a team is going forward. The Bot plays out the rest of the season 10,000 times, seats each title game from the simulated standings, then picks the playoff field the same way as above. "Bowl teams" is how many teams the conference can expect to finish bowl-eligible, and "Playoff bids" is how many it can expect in the 12-team field. The two can disagree with the bracket: a team with a strong résumé but a hard schedule left can rank high in the Index and still have long playoff odds.
