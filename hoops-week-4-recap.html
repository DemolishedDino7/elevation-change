The Western 23: Week 4 Basketball Recap

Oregon State takes down Oregon to stay perfect, Texas State moves to 7–0, Wyoming climbs to 5–1, UNLV drops three straight in Las Vegas, and UTEP emerges as an early Mountain West surprise.

By Tyler Angerbauer | Nov 24, 2026 | 23 teams, six conferences

The fourth week of college basketball delivered some significant developments across Elevation Change’s 23-team coverage. Oregon State remained undefeated with an impressive victory over in-state rival Oregon, while Texas State continued its perfect start with two convincing wins. Wyoming quietly improved to 5–1, but New Mexico suffered another setback in a blowout loss to Wichita State. Elsewhere, UNLV’s early momentum evaporated during a winless three-game stretch, and UTEP emerged as one of the Mountain West’s early surprises.

Let’s see what happened in the week before Thanksgiving tournaments.

Pac-12

Gonzaga

Season 3–1 | This week 1–0

Best game
74–62 win vs. Montana State
Games and results
  • Nov. 17: W 74–62 vs. Montana State

Gonzaga returned to the win column following its heartbreaking one-point loss to LSU, defeating Montana State 74–62 in Spokane. The Bulldogs faced a more competitive challenge than expected from the Bobcats, but managed to secure a 12-point victory to improve to 3–1 overall. After averaging nearly 90 points through their first three games, Gonzaga’s offense was held to its lowest scoring total of the season. Although the victory was not particularly dominant, Mark Few’s team demonstrated its ability to bounce back from an early setback. With high-profile matchups against Kansas State and potential opponents Alabama or Baylor approaching in Las Vegas, the Bulldogs will have another opportunity to demonstrate their national championship potential.

Utah State

Season 5–0 | This week 1–0

Best game
101–71 win vs. Weber State
Games and results
  • Nov. 18: W 101–71 vs. Weber State

Utah State continued its remarkable offensive start to the season with a commanding 101–71 victory over in-state opponent Weber State. The Aggies surpassed the century mark for the third consecutive game, improving to 5–0 under first-year head coach Ben Jacobson. Will Hornseth led the Aggies with 22 points and eight rebounds, while Dallin Hall added nine assists in another impressive offensive showing. Utah State has now won all five of its games by at least 30 points, outscoring its opponents by a combined 170 points. Although the Aggies have yet to face a nationally recognized contender, their offensive efficiency has made them one of the Pac-12’s most intriguing early-season teams. More demanding challenges against SMU, USC and New Mexico await in the coming weeks.

Oregon State

Season 6–0 | This week 2–0

Best game
82–71 win vs. Oregon
Games and results
  • Nov. 16: W 109–71 vs. East Texas A&M
  • Nov. 19: W 82–71 vs. Oregon (Portland, Oregon)

Oregon State continued its perfect start to the Justin Joyner era with two victories, highlighted by an impressive win over in-state rival Oregon. The Beavers began the week by overwhelming East Texas A&M 109–71, with Daniel Freitag scoring 23 points in the victory. Three days later, Oregon State traveled to Portland for a highly anticipated rivalry matchup against the Ducks, emerging with an 82–71 victory. The win represented the Beavers’ most significant result of the young season and provided an early statement about their potential in the rebuilt Pac-12. Oregon State has now scored at least 81 points in every game while remaining undefeated through six contests. After entering the season with modest expectations, the Beavers have quickly established themselves as one of the conference’s most surprising early success stories.

Boise State

Season 3–2 | This week 0–1

Best game
83–69 loss vs. Sam Houston
Games and results
  • Nov. 20: L 69–83 vs. Sam Houston

Boise State’s promising offensive start has quickly turned into a concerning losing streak, as the Broncos suffered an 83–69 home defeat against Sam Houston. After scoring at least 100 points in each of their first three games, the Broncos have now been held below 76 points in consecutive losses. The setback against Sam Houston was particularly disappointing after Boise State’s lopsided defeat to Nebraska the previous week, raising questions about the team’s ability to generate consistent offense against competitive Division I opponents. Leon Rice’s team has dropped to 3–2 after beginning the season undefeated, and the Broncos will need to rediscover the offensive rhythm that characterized their opening performances. With Pac-12 competition approaching, Boise State’s early inconsistency could become a significant concern.

Colorado State

Season 4–1 | This week 1–0

Best game
71–64 win vs. North Dakota State
Games and results
  • Nov. 18: W 71–64 vs. North Dakota State

Colorado State bounced back from its first loss of the season with a hard-fought 71–64 victory over North Dakota State. After struggling offensively against Loyola Chicago the previous week, the Rams managed to generate enough production to defeat the defending Summit League champions. Although Colorado State did not showcase the explosive offense that characterized its first three victories, its ability to win a tighter contest provides an encouraging sign for Ali Farokhmanesh’s squad. The Rams improved to 4–1 heading into the Maui Invitational, where they will face a considerably more demanding level of competition. With nationally relevant opponents awaiting them in Hawaii, Colorado State will soon have an opportunity to evaluate its ability to compete against some of college basketball’s established powers.

Texas State

Season 7–0 | This week 2–0

Best game
95–69 win vs. McNeese
Games and results
  • Nov. 18: W 84–51 vs. Incarnate Word
  • Nov. 22: W 95–69 vs. McNeese

Texas State continued its undefeated start with two dominant victories, improving to 7–0 in its inaugural Pac-12 campaign. The Bobcats opened the week with an 84–51 win over Incarnate Word before delivering a convincing 95–69 performance against McNeese. Texas State has now won all seven of its games by at least seven points, demonstrating impressive consistency for a program adjusting to a major conference transition. The victory against McNeese was particularly encouraging, as the Bobcats overwhelmed a program with recent NCAA Tournament experience. While Texas State still faces questions about how its performance will translate against the Pac-12’s strongest teams, its perfect start is becoming increasingly difficult to overlook. The Bobcats have established themselves as one of the conference’s most surprising early-season stories.

San Diego State

Season 5–0 | This week 1–0

Best game
97–51 win vs. Texas Southern
Games and results
  • Nov. 18: W 97–51 vs. Texas Southern

San Diego State continued its undefeated start with its most dominant performance of the season, dismantling Texas Southern 97–51 at Viejas Arena. The Aztecs controlled the game on both ends of the floor, holding the Tigers to just 51 points while scoring 97 themselves. The 46-point margin marked San Diego State’s largest victory of the young campaign and demonstrated the defensive intensity that has long defined Brian Dutcher’s program. The Aztecs have now won five consecutive games, including victories over Colorado and New Mexico State, while continuing to develop a more efficient offensive attack. With Gonzaga and Utah State also enjoying strong starts, San Diego State appears positioned to play a significant role in what could become a highly competitive Pac-12 championship race.

Fresno State

Season 4–2 | This week 1–1

Best game
92–86 loss at San Jose State
Games and results
  • Nov. 18: L 86–92 at San Jose State
  • Nov. 20: W 80–68 vs. Alabama State

Fresno State experienced another mixed week, splitting its games against San Jose State and Alabama State to improve to 4–2 overall. The Bulldogs began with a disappointing 92–86 road loss against their former Mountain West rival, allowing the Spartans to generate consistent scoring opportunities throughout the contest. Fresno State responded two days later with a more controlled 80–68 victory over Alabama State, securing its fourth win of the season. Although the Bulldogs have demonstrated the ability to score, they have now surrendered at least 80 points in four of their six games, raising concerns about their defensive consistency. Fresno State has shown encouraging offensive potential during its first Pac-12 campaign, but significant improvements on the defensive end will be necessary to compete with the conference’s top teams.

Washington State

Season 2–4 | This week 1–1

Best game
78–74 loss vs. Duke
Games and results
  • Nov. 18: W 79–63 vs. Hawaii (Everett, Washington)
  • Nov. 21: L 74–78 vs. Duke (Palm Desert, California)

Washington State delivered its most encouraging performances of the season, splitting two games against Hawaii and Duke. The Cougars opened the week with a convincing 79–63 victory over Hawaii, securing their second consecutive win after a difficult 0–3 start. Washington State then faced its most significant challenge of the young season against Duke, pushing the Blue Devils throughout the contest before falling 78–74. Although the Cougars were unable to complete the upset, their competitive performance against one of college basketball’s most recognizable programs represents an important sign of progress. After struggling through their opening games, Washington State has demonstrated improved competitiveness and offensive consistency. The Cougars may still face an uphill battle in the Pac-12, but this week’s results provide reason to believe the team is beginning to find its rhythm.

Mountain West

New Mexico

Season 3–2 | This week 0–1

Best game
85–60 loss at Wichita State
Games and results
  • Nov. 21: L 60–85 at Wichita State

New Mexico suffered its second consecutive defeat, falling 85–60 to Wichita State in a disappointing road performance. After opening the season with three dominant victories, the Lobos have struggled against stronger competition, losing to Santa Clara and Wichita State by a combined 30 points. The 60-point outing against the Shockers represented New Mexico’s lowest offensive output of the season, a concerning development for a team that averaged 93.3 points through its first four contests. Eric Olen’s squad entered the year as the Mountain West preseason favorite, but its recent performances have raised questions about the team’s ability to translate its early offensive success against quality opponents. With a highly anticipated matchup against former conference rival Utah State approaching, New Mexico will have an important opportunity to regain momentum and strengthen its postseason résumé.

Grand Canyon

Season 4–0 | This week 0–0

Best game
No games played
Games and results
  • No games scheduled November 16–22.

Grand Canyon enjoyed a break from competition after opening the season with four consecutive victories. The Lopes entered the week undefeated, having won each of their games by at least 18 points while showcasing an impressive combination of offensive production and defensive intensity. Bryce Drew’s team has averaged more than 97 points per game through its opening four contests, emerging as one of the Mountain West’s most dangerous early-season programs. However, the Antelopes have yet to face a major test against nationally relevant competition, leaving questions about how their dominant performances will translate against stronger opponents. With New Mexico and Nevada already suffering multiple defeats, Grand Canyon has an opportunity to establish itself as an early conference favorite when its schedule resumes.

Nevada

Season 3–2 | This week 1–0

Best game
91–74 win vs. Idaho
Games and results
  • Nov. 18: W 91–74 vs. Idaho

Nevada returned to the win column with an impressive offensive performance, defeating Idaho 91–74 at Lawlor Events Center. The victory snapped a two-game losing streak that followed the Wolf Pack’s thrilling overtime victory against Saint Mary’s earlier in the season. Steve Alford’s team rediscovered its offensive rhythm against the Vandals, shooting better than 55 percent from the field while knocking down 11 three-pointers. The performance provided a welcome turnaround after Nevada struggled to score consistently in defeats against UC Santa Barbara and Utah Valley. Although the Wolf Pack have shown considerable offensive potential, finding consistency against stronger opponents remains an important challenge. Nevada will have another opportunity to build its postseason résumé during the upcoming Acrisure Invitational in Palm Desert.

Wyoming

Season 5–1 | This week 2–0

Best game
79–61 win at Duquesne
Games and results
  • Nov. 17: W 81–79 vs. Sam Houston
  • Nov. 22: W 79–61 at Duquesne (Daytona Beach, Florida)

Wyoming continued its impressive start with consecutive victories over Sam Houston and Duquesne, improving to 5–1 overall. The Cowboys opened their week with a narrow 81–79 victory against Sam Houston, surviving a tightly contested matchup to secure their fourth win of the season. Wyoming followed that performance with an impressive 79–61 victory over Duquesne at a neutral-site event in Daytona Beach, demonstrating its ability to win away from Laramie. The Cowboys connected on 12 three-pointers against the Dukes, highlighting an improved perimeter attack that could become an important weapon throughout conference play. Sundance Wicks’ team has now won three consecutive games following its lone defeat at North Carolina. With several Mountain West contenders experiencing early struggles, Wyoming is quietly emerging as one of the conference’s most encouraging teams.

UNLV

Season 3–3 | This week 0–3

Best game
86–71 loss vs. Auburn
Games and results
  • Nov. 17: L 77–102 vs. Kansas (Las Vegas)
  • Nov. 18: L 71–86 vs. Auburn (Las Vegas)
  • Nov. 19: L 70–89 vs. Rutgers (Las Vegas)

UNLV’s undefeated start came to an abrupt end as the Runnin’ Rebels suffered three consecutive defeats against major-conference competition. After opening the season with victories over Northern Arizona, Samford and Stanford, Josh Pastner’s squad encountered a considerably more difficult stretch against Kansas, Auburn and Rutgers. The Rebels were overwhelmed in a 102–77 defeat against the Jayhawks before suffering losses of 15 and 19 points against Auburn and Rutgers, respectively. UNLV allowed an average of more than 92 points across the three contests while struggling to maintain the offensive production that characterized its opening victories. The difficult week exposed significant concerns about the team’s ability to defend and compete against stronger opponents. Although the Rebels remain capable of challenging in the Mountain West, their recent performances demonstrate how much improvement will be necessary to return to NCAA Tournament contention.

Hawaii

Season 3–2 | This week 0–1

Best game
79–63 loss vs. Washington State
Games and results
  • Nov. 18: L 63–79 vs. Washington State (Everett, Washington)

Hawaii suffered its second consecutive defeat, falling 79–63 to Washington State in a neutral-site matchup in Everett. The Rainbow Warriors struggled to find consistent offensive production against the Cougars, scoring just 63 points after being held to 66 in their previous defeat against Portland. After beginning the season with three consecutive victories, Eran Ganot’s team has now dropped back-to-back contests while averaging just 64.5 points during that stretch. Hawaii’s offensive struggles have become a concerning trend for a program looking to build on last season’s NCAA Tournament appearance. The Rainbow Warriors have demonstrated the ability to compete in close games, but developing a more reliable offensive attack will be essential against the deeper competition awaiting them in the Mountain West.

UC Davis

Best game

Games and results

UC Davis suffered a disappointing setback against Alabama State, falling 75–68 at home and snapping its three-game winning streak. Air Force suffered another disappointing road defeat, falling 90–68 to UTSA in San Antonio. UTEP

Season 4–1 | This week 2–0

Best game
84–69 win at New Mexico State
Games and results
  • Nov. 16: W 79–46 vs. Florida A&M
  • Nov. 19: W 84–69 at New Mexico State

UTEP emerged as one of the Mountain West’s most encouraging early-season surprises, earning consecutive victories over Florida A&M and New Mexico State to improve to 4–1. The Miners began their week with a dominant 79–46 win against Florida A&M, limiting the Rattlers to just 46 points while connecting on 13 three-pointers. UTEP then traveled to Las Cruces for its second rivalry matchup against New Mexico State, delivering a convincing 84–69 victory after winning the first meeting by just two points. Joe Golding’s team has now won four consecutive games following its season-opening defeat, demonstrating significant improvement on both ends of the floor. With Nevada, New Mexico and UNLV all experiencing early setbacks, UTEP has positioned itself as an intriguing contender in the Mountain West’s evolving championship picture.

San Jose State

Season 3–3 | This week 1–1

Best game
92–86 win vs. Fresno State
Games and results
  • Nov. 18: W 92–86 vs. Fresno State
  • Nov. 22: L 63–72 vs. Loyola Chicago (Palm Desert, California)

San Jose State experienced a mixed week, earning an impressive victory over former conference rival Fresno State before falling to Loyola Chicago. The Spartans opened with a 92–86 home win against the Bulldogs, demonstrating their offensive capabilities against a Pac-12 opponent. San Jose State shot 50 percent from the field and knocked down 12 three-pointers in the victory, providing one of its most complete offensive performances of the season. However, the Spartans were unable to carry that momentum into their neutral-site matchup against Loyola Chicago, falling 72–63 after struggling to generate consistent scoring opportunities. Tim Miles’ team has now alternated between impressive offensive performances and frustrating setbacks, highlighting the challenge of establishing consistency. If San Jose State can replicate its showing against Fresno State, the Spartans could become a difficult opponent in Mountain West play.

Games and results
  • Nov. 18: L 64–71 at Colorado State

North Dakota State’s difficult start continued with a 71–64 road defeat against Colorado State, dropping the defending Summit League champions to 2–5 overall. Horizon League

Best game
86–68 win at Bowling Green
Games and results
  • Nov. 18: L 65–79 at Marquette
  • Nov. 21: W 86–68 at Bowling Green

Northern Illinois continued its encouraging start with a split week against Marquette and Bowling Green, improving to 4–3 overall. The Huskies began with a challenging road matchup against Big East opponent Marquette, suffering a 79–65 defeat that snapped their three-game winning streak. Big West

Games and results

Sacramento State’s difficult start continued with a 78–65 road defeat against Stanford, extending the Hornets’ losing streak to three games. With conference play approaching, Sacramento State faces an important opportunity to establish greater consistency and build momentum after a frustrating opening stretch.
