New Mexico State vs. Sam Houston

New Mexico State 2-4 (1-1), Sam Houston 1-5 (0-2)

New Mexico State takes on Sam Houston in a Conference USA matchup between two teams looking to turn their seasons around. The Aggies enter at 2-4 overall and 1-1 in conference play, while the Bearkats are 1-5 and still searching for their first conference win. Neither team has been particularly consistent, but New Mexico State has a chance to take advantage of a struggling Sam Houston squad.

Both teams have had trouble generating points, making offensive execution a major factor in this game. New Mexico State will need to improve its ability to sustain drives and finish scoring opportunities, while Sam Houston must find a way to get its offense moving after several disappointing performances. The team that makes fewer mistakes and takes advantage of its opportunities should have the upper hand.

We give New Mexico State the edge because Sam Houston’s offensive struggles have been difficult to overlook. The Boys are predicting a comfortable Aggies victory, while the Bot expects a much closer contest. We think New Mexico State will find enough offense to win without allowing Sam Houston to make things interesting late.

Sacramento State vs. Ball State

Sacramento State 1-6 (0-4), Ball State 2-5 (1-2)

Ladies and gentlemen, this is the matchup you’ve all been waiting for: the Ball-Sac Bowl.

Sacramento State makes the 1,912-mile trip across the country to take on Ball State in a battle of MAC nether-dwellers. The Hornets are at the very bottom of nearly every FBS statistic in their first season at the FBS level, but they’ll hope to iron out the wrinkles against an only slightly better Ball State squad.

It’s been a fairly blue season so far for the Cardinals, but they scored a ballsy 2-point try in overtime to secure their first conference and FBS win of the season in a 32-31 victory over Bowling Green.

Both the Hornets and the Cardinals have struggled to make adjustments most of the season. Both teams have regularly found themselves in ESPN’s Bottom 10 list this season, so a victory for either squad would create loads of positive momentum.

Sacramento State hasn’t scored more than 10 points in a game since its Week 2 victory over FCS Mississippi Valley State. It’ll be hard-pressed for them to bust out more than two scores, even against a lowly Ball State team.

Both The Boys and The Bot think that this game will end in a blowout. Perhaps the winner of this game will gain a sponsorship deal with Manscaped or Fruit of the Loom; I guess we’ll just have to wait and see.

San Diego State vs. Colorado State

San Diego State 3-4 (2-1), Colorado State 2-4 (0-2)

San Diego State has been a victim of facing teams at their strongest this season. Of their four losses this season, the teams they lost to have a combined 23-4 record, including two teams, UCLA and James Madison, who have yet to lose a game this season. Despite that, the Aztecs have navigated an insane amount of injuries and a team mumps outbreak to sit tied for third in the Pac-12 with four games to go.

The Aztecs lost quarterback Jayden Denegal to an ankle injury in the first half of their win against Fresno State, but backup freshman Stone Saunders filled in admirably, going 6-of-11 passing for 61 yards and leading the Aztecs to kick the game-winning field goal.

Colorado State has had a good offense this season, but its defense ranks dead last in yards and points given up per game. The Rams have already fired their defensive coordinator this season, midway through their loss against Oregon State, but it seems the change in leadership did not lead to much change as they proceeded to give up 716 total yards against Texas State, who now sit as the best offense across the FBS in yards per game.

The Boys and The Bot think that this will be a close, high-scoring game. Both teams have been wildly inconsistent this season, but give us the Aztecs on the road as the slightest of favorites.

vs.

North Dakota State vs. New Mexico

North Dakota State 6-1 (3-1), New Mexico 5-1 (2-0)

In our Elevation Change Game of the Week, North Dakota State and New Mexico face off in the battle of the top Mountain West contenders and a potential step towards the G6’s automatic playoff bid.

The Lobos are coming off a disappointing performance at Hawai‘i, scoring only 21 points, all in the second half, their second-lowest of the season. Despite the underwhelming performance, they were able to still put up 243 yards on the ground, their third-best performance of the season. Scottre Humphrey led the way with 90 yards and two touchdowns, while quarterback Toa Fa’avae added 77 yards and a touchdown.

Interestingly, the Lobos and Bison sit as the number one and number two teams in the entire country in points allowed per game this season, at 10.5 and 11.3, respectively. Both teams have been dominant on the defensive front, and the battle over which defense will prevail will be a key factor in deciding this game.

North Dakota State is coming off a closer game than they were expecting against a pesky Nevada squad. The Bison needed the efforts of their special teams to secure the victory, scoring both a 100-yard missed field goal return touchdown and a defensive two-point conversion against the Wolfpack.

Despite both teams’ defense being the stars of the show, we think this will come down to which offense can get the last push. We’re going to lean toward the home Lobos in this matchup that could determine the Mountain West title.
